OK, Let's Go.

From the street, to the entry, to the spaces... the building is nice but unnoticed. Everything is new, nice and polished. It doesn't feel dated, off trend or cheap. It just doesn't feel, anything.

The opportunity, as we know, is to give it a story – physical to mood – that writes a script, dresses it in a look and creates a feel.

Opportunities

  • Street presence. Announcing the building. A residence, for rent, available now, how to reach us. Signage that says here we are. Hello!
  • Sense of arrival. Organic planting, vibrancy, bikes at the door and hospitality cues that call attention from the street.
  • The entry is chapter one. A first impression that welcomes and sets the tone.
  • A lobby that's lived in. The greeting, the scent, the sound, a coffee, a soft recline, other people and a reason to stay.
  • Amenities with a point of view. Venture on and the style carries with you. One floor, everything on it. Pool, fitness and lounges styled as moments, not checklists.
  • The details. The sounds, the scents, the nuances. Books on the shelves, art in the hallway, games on the table. A night at the Marriott, or my home?
  • A material story. Warm oak, linen, thick rugs, barefoot feels, soft seating, candles and vibe lighting, Nordic roots made modern.
  • Echoed in sound. From a background playlist to a vinyl lounge, proper speakers that are seen as well as heard, and a B&O soundbath in the yoga studio.
  • Considered art. Framed photos, where the frames matter equally. Discreet but intentional pieces, many small but composed moments, authored and signed. Larger installations in larger spaces, like the garage.

Section 3.0 · Concept 01

Modern Heritage

Concept 01 · Scandinavian Vibes

A literal nod to the contrasting architecture along with the city's Scandinavian heritage. This theme takes cues from Nordic and design sensibilities to create a modern retreat that is both meaningful and memorable.

The vibe is hygge, but styled. An overlay that uses the existing spaces as a base and updates them with the warm woods, fabrics, textures and light of contemporary Scandi design.

Concept 01 · The Vibe

Nordic Modern,
City Meets Hygge

The Scandi influence of clean lines and calm moods. Natural woods and fabrics, warm light and soft layers. Modern yet welcoming, focused on small moments and thoughtful gatherings.

Idea 01 · Corridor Artwork

Installations, not murals

Art integrated with the texture of materials of the building, not overt paint of random photos set on top of it. Physical, sculptural, made by hand — a few serious pieces placed where people actually pass, so the building collects rather than decorates.

Relief tile wall · ceramic, built into the architecture
Wall relief · the entry hall
Ceramic assemblage · a composed wall
Stacked ceramic totem · the courtyard
Woven fiber · sculptural, room-scale
Fiber hanging · quiet, made by hand

Idea 02 · Candles, Scent and the Coffee Bar

The Entry

The first moments inside: the scent, lit candles, mood and light, a soundtrack, a concierge who is also the barista — the hello, the drink offered, the name remembered.

The building should smell like somewhere. D.S. & Durga or Loewe, distinct not off the shelf. And aligned with the texture, tile, lighting and mood — so that coming home has a sense.

The scent · Le Labo Papyrus 20, the lit match
The mood · Loewe home scents, ceramic, botanicals
The air · palo santo, smoke and wood
The pour · a flat white, made by hand
The bar · light, stone, the machine
The hello · concierge and barista, the name remembered

Idea 03 · Light

The Light

Light as a mood. Showing material not reflecting or glossing over it. Low, warm and layered — a soft glow, daylight pulled deep into the room by morning or late shadows showing layers by evening. Nothing harsh, nothing overhead, nothing direct.

Pendants over the long table
Sconces, a lamp, the fire
Living, dining, kitchen in one warm light
Morning sun across the counter
Daylight deep into the plan
Sauna · light along the slats
Warm wood, a glow beneath the cabinets
Arched windows, pendants over the island
